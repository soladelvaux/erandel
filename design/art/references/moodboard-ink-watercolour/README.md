# Mood board: ink and watercolour (for later)

**Added**: 2026-11-06, by Sola, during Phase 7 QA.
**Status**: mood board only. **Nothing in the game changes because of it yet.** The current art bible (`ERANDEL_ART_BIBLE.md`, decisions D1–D3) stays in force until Sola decides otherwise.

## What Sola chose
- These pictures are a **mood board for later**. No change during Phase 7.
- When the direction is taken up, it should reach **the world and magic too**, not only characters: ink-wash ground and skies, creatures and spells made of splashing ink, and a stronger miniature-diorama focus blur.

## What the pictures show
| File | What to take from it |
|---|---|
| `ref_mood_ink-dragon-diorama.png` | A small character in a teal cloak casting a spark at a dragon made of splashing blue and black ink. Sumi-e brush landscape on paper. Tilt-shift depth of field, a table-top diorama feel, and warm light on cream paper. |
| `ref_mood_watercolour-dragon-studio.png` | A small painter beside a watercolour dragon (teal, orange, cream) that rises off the canvas in loose splashes. A painter's table, bokeh lanterns, warm interior light. |

**Shared traits:**
- Loose ink outlines and watercolour fills, with paper texture showing through.
- Creatures and magic that dissolve into splashes and drips at their edges.
- Muted teal, cream and rust palette with one warm accent light.
- Shallow focus (miniature effect).

## Open question for the day this is taken up
The characters in these pictures are about **3 heads tall** (chibi). Decision **D1** retired chibi, and **D2** set Aelarion's taller anime proportions as the style reference. Sola must decide the proportions again before any character work starts from this board.
