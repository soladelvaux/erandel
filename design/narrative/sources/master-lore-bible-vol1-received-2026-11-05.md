### PART I — THE IDENTITY OF ERANDEL

### 2\. The world

ESTABLISHED

Erandel is your original fantasy universe and the foundation of a game development project.

The project already uses Three.js, with a previous technical audit identifying an older 2021 version. Our development direction is to modernize the existing engine and organize the game architecture rather than replace the entire engine.

PROPOSED — WORLD IDENTITY

Erandel is an ancient world where civilization exists upon the remains of forgotten civilizations.

Its inhabitants believe they understand their world's history. They have religions, kingdoms, libraries, monuments and legends.

But their understanding is incomplete.

The ruins beneath their cities are older than their oldest recorded kings. Some artifacts predate known magical disciplines. Certain creatures remember events that no living historian can explain.

The central mystery of Erandel is:

What happened to the world before its recorded history began?

### The five pillars of Erandel

* Ancient mystery — The past is never entirely dead.

* Consequence — Every civilization inherits the consequences of those before it.

* Discovery — Exploration reveals stories rather than merely rewards.

* Power — Magic always has limitations and consequences.

* Legacy — Even forgotten people can change the future.

### 3\. The central narrative philosophy

PROPOSED

Erandel should not have a simplistic division between good and evil.

A kingdom may preserve civilization while concealing a terrible historical crime.

A necromancer may be considered evil because of the magic they practice, while secretly protecting the boundary between life and something much worse.

An ancient god may have created humanity for reasons that humanity would find unacceptable.

A monster may be the last surviving witness to a forgotten catastrophe.

This creates opportunities for complex stories across games, novels and cinematic episodes.

### PART II — COSMOLOGY AND CREATION

### 4\. The beginning of existence

PROPOSED — NEW MYTHOLOGY

The following is a proposed foundational creation myth. It can become canon after approval.

![Images of Glass sphere shattered on wooden floor, cosmic universe captured within, colorful galaxies and stars, intricate details,... | Deep Dream Generator](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/-_qeEBWQBk328GJNmxqHeReDCQmsTcM8tiWEdCkijdyoCgQR4cTJqOacwP3fGZ-i7A5NH44t01dlWuPYP0kM8OLGS02goF-CUaLPJIhNfCCoBwrhuNH0zWI9hI6oSIKlf8tTLPv-Uyy0vCmpC_jK5jh0qjc5ZSLEwTdTfs0uM1K8tVCjw-P4JFGpHfZd_JHX?purpose=fullsize)

Before Erandel, there was no sky, no ground, no time and no death.

There was only the First Silence.

The First Silence was not an empty space. It was a state in which everything that could ever exist remained undifferentiated.

Then came the First Awakening.

Something within the Silence became aware of itself.

From that awareness emerged three primordial principles:

| Principle  | Meaning                                               |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| The Origin | Creation, existence and possibility                   |
| The Veil   | Boundaries, memory and the passage between states     |
| The End    | Decay, transformation and the conclusion of existence |

These are not necessarily gods. They are fundamental forces.

Their interaction created the first distinctions: light and darkness, matter and spirit, existence and absence.

Eventually, the material world emerged.

It became known as Erandel.

### 5\. The Three Primordial Principles

PROPOSED

The Origin — The Beginning

The force responsible for creation, growth and the formation of new existence.

The Veil — The Boundary

The force separating different states of existence. It defines the boundary between physical and spiritual realities.

The End — The Transformation

The force through which things cease to exist in their current form. It governs endings, decay and transformation.

An important distinction: The End is not inherently evil. Without endings, nothing could change.

This is especially important for the future necromancer mythology.

### PART III — THE HISTORY OF ERANDEL

### 7\. The grand chronology

PROPOSED — HISTORICAL FRAMEWORK

The history of Erandel will be organized into seven ages.

### The Seven Ages

* The Age Before Time

    The primordial forces emerge. Existence takes its first form.

* The Age of First Creation

    The world takes shape. The earliest beings emerge and the foundations of magic are established.

* The Age of Ancients

    The first civilizations rise. Knowledge, architecture, religion and magical practices develop.

* The Age of Ascendancy

    Great empires compete for power. Magical knowledge reaches extraordinary heights.

* The Age of Sundering

    A catastrophic conflict changes the world. Civilizations collapse and historical knowledge disappears.

* The Age of Reclamation

    New kingdoms emerge from the ruins. Religion, politics and magic develop independently.

* The Age of the Present

    The world appears stable, but the ancient past begins resurfacing.

### The defining historical mystery

PROPOSED

The Age of Sundering should become the most important historical event in Erandel.

Its precise cause remains deliberately unresolved.

Different civilizations preserve contradictory accounts:

* Some believe the ancient empires fought over magical supremacy.

* Others believe the gods abandoned the world.

* Some religious institutions teach that the catastrophe was divine punishment.

* Necromantic traditions claim the boundary between life and death was damaged.

* Ancient inscriptions suggest that the catastrophe may have been an attempt to prevent something from entering the world.

The player should gradually discover that these accounts are fragments of a much larger truth.

### 10\. Politics and power

PROPOSED

The political structure should include five major power centers:

* Royal dynasties — Control territories and military forces.

* Religious institutions — Interpret the divine and preserve sacred knowledge.

* Arcane orders — Regulate magical knowledge and research.

* Merchant alliances — Control trade, resources and transportation.

* Independent factions — Adventurers, mercenaries, explorers and underground organizations.

This creates a world in which the player can encounter competing versions of the truth.

### 12\. The laws of magic

To keep the game and stories consistent, I recommend five laws.

Law I — Conservation: Magic cannot create unlimited existence from nothing.

Law II — Cost: Powerful magic requires a sacrifice, resource or consequence.

Law III — Resistance: Reality resists unnatural manipulation.

Law IV — Knowledge: Understanding determines what a practitioner can safely manipulate.

Law V — Irreversibility: Some magical consequences cannot be undone.

The fifth law is particularly important for necromancy.

In Erandel, death is not simply the opposite of life.

Death is a transition.

When a living being dies, its physical body returns to the material world. Its spiritual essence crosses the Veil.

But not every passage is completed.

Some memories remain attached to places. Some spirits remain bound to objects. Some souls become trapped by unfinished obligations, unnatural rituals or disturbances in the boundary between worlds.

Necromancy emerges from the study of these phenomena.

### Three forms of necromancy

PROPOSED

| Discipline        | Description                                                |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spirit Communion  | Communicating with remnants of the departed                |
| Corporeal Binding | Reanimating or controlling physical remains                |
| Soul Manipulation | Interfering with the essence of a living or departed being |

The third is considered the most dangerous.

### The forbidden question

What happens when someone successfully returns from death?

There should be no simple answer.

Some return with their memories.

Some return incomplete.

Some are not the people they claim to be.

And some may bring something back with them.
